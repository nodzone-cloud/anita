S.P.A.C.E. 2.4.9 - Self Permission / Admin Safety Fix

- Administrators no longer see clickable controls for their own permissions.
- Their own permissions are shown read-only as a summary.
- Administrators cannot change their own system role or permissions.
- Only Owner can change system roles and permission grants.
- Owner and Technical Administrator remain protected.
- Account lifecycle actions remain Owner-only.
- Existing LAN, chat/file, event, management and role-permission fixes are preserved.
