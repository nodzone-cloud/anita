S.P.A.C.E. 2.4.9 ROLE/PERMISSIONS FIX

- Changing a user to Administrator now grants real business capabilities, not only a role label.
- Administrator defaults: users, materials/content, events, files, school, onboarding.
- Owner can still disable individual Administrator permissions.
- Changing back to Team Member clears administrator permissions.
- LAN clients refresh live role/permissions from /api/me before opening a section and every 5 seconds while running.
- Management shows School and Onboarding permission switches for Administrator.
- Position/job title remains separate from System Role and grants no permissions by itself.
