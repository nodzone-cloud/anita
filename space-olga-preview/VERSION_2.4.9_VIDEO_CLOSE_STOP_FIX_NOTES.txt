S.P.A.C.E. 2.4.9 - VIDEO CLOSE STOP FIX

Fix:
- Closing a News detail modal now immediately stops any playing video/audio.
- HTML5 video/audio is paused and reset.
- Embedded iframe players (YouTube/Vimeo/Dailymotion/other embedded sites) are destroyed by clearing their source before the modal closes.
- Clicking outside the News modal uses the same safe close path, so playback also stops there.
- Existing Universal News Video features and all previous fixes remain unchanged.
