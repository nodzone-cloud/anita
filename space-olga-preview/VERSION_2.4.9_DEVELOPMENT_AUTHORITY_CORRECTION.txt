S.P.A.C.E. 2.4.9 — Development Authority Correction

Corrected the permission model after testing:
- Alex Node / Technical Administrator has development-time Management access for ordinary accounts.
- Technical Administrator can invite users, switch Team Member <-> Administrator, assign/revoke ordinary admin permissions, reset passwords, suspend/restore/remove ordinary accounts, and manage/test business modules.
- Existing ordinary Administrator accounts that accidentally inherited the full developer permission set are reset once to no optional admin permissions. This corrects Oleg's account after upgrade.
- Promoting a Team Member to Administrator no longer automatically grants every permission. Permissions are separate and must be assigned explicitly.
- Owner and Technical Administrator accounts remain protected from ordinary account management.
- Technical Administrator does not become Owner.
- Private-chat privacy rules are unchanged.
- General-chat support access remains separately controlled.
